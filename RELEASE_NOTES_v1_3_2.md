## Overview

This release closes one of the corpus's oldest open bridges: the microscopic
Hawking mechanism. Doc. 313 (Ch. G) had laid the thermodynamic frame — the single
KMS rule for Unruh, Gibbons-Hawking and black holes — but booked the emission side
as a sketch [S]. Doc. 325 works it out completely from FFGFT-native building blocks:
temperature, quantum, selection, carrier and information conservation now stand at
[K]/[B] (register R85).

The central new finding: **the Hawking quantum is the system-dependent bit of the
horizon scale** — k_B·T_H = ħc/(4π·r_s) = E_bit(L = 4π·r_s) exactly. The FFGFT
position that bit values are system-dependent (Docs. 257, 302, A271–A273) is thus
no obstacle to Hawking physics but its explanation. A universal bit value is
needed nowhere.

The mechanism is flanked by two comparison documents on Douglas Matzke's algebra
frameworks: Doc. 324 (G(6,ℤ₃C) vacuum structure, with the Casimir derivation
ξ = C₂(SU(3))/N_Fourier, register R84) and Doc. 326 (hyperbit black-hole physics,
focus on Landauer and bit values). Both document remarkable structural
convergences — and the one deep divergence: universal vs. system-dependent
bit values.

No source document was modified; all refinements enter via the append-only
correction register (Doc. 190, R84–R85).
