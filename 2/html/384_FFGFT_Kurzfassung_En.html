1 What FFGFT is

1.1 Ratios in natural units

The core of FFGFT is ratios. In natural units, mass, energy, temperature and inverse time are one quantity in four guises (A085), and every physical statement can be written as a dimensionless ratio: mass ratios, scale ratios to the Planck quantities, mixing angles, charge ratios. 1.4 Guidelines

Five rules determine how results are to be read:

  1. Ratios are what counts. An absolute value in SI tests the conversion, not the structure (A130).

  2. FFGFT works with ξ\xi alone. Measured values from SI and the Standard Model serve only for conversion and comparison, and for that exactly one measured value is needed as anchor (Doc. 383).

The numbers test these explanations; they do not replace the calculations of the Standard Model.
