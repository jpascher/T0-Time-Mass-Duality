\subsection{Mathematical foundation: Möbius energy}

\textbf{[K]} The Möbius energy is mathematically rigorously defined
(Freedman, He \& Wang 1994):
\begin{equation}
  E_{\text{Möb}}(\gamma) = \iint
  \left(\frac{1}{|\gamma(s)-\gamma(t)|^2}
  - \frac{1}{d_\gamma(\gamma(s),\gamma(t))^2}\right)
  |\gamma'(s)||\gamma'(t)|\,\mathrm{d}s\,\mathrm{d}t
\end{equation}
It is conformally invariant and possesses minimisers in every knot
class. Blatt et al. (2025) prove: in every torus knot class $T(a,b)$
for co-prime $a,b$ there exist at least two distinct critical points
of the Möbius energy.
