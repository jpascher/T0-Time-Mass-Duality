\section{Open Questions in the Hyperbit--FFGFT Connection}

\textbf{Cl(6) and GF(27).}
Is the Clifford algebra $\mathrm{Cl}(6)$ a special case of the FFGFT Galois structure
on $T^4$, or are both independent paths to the same physics? If there is a
mapping: What are the translation rules?

\textbf{Universal bit mass.}
Matzke's $\mbit = \mP \ln 2 / (2\pi)$ is a universal constant ---
in the FFGFT it is scale-sensitive. Is there a range in which both
predictions are experimentally distinguishable?

\textbf{p-bit prediction.}
FFGFT says: natural p-bit materials at $L_8 \approx 22\,\text{nm}$.
Are there measurements that show this scale as particularly stable or unstable?

\section{Open Questions in the Landauer Interpretation}

\textbf{Multiple-realization invariance (Doc.\,A272).}
Are there physical systems in which the invariance breaks --- systems in
which the energy varies with the information content through constructive
coupling? That would touch the proof of the carrier theorem, but not
its premise.

\textbf{Phase dissipation.}
Is the information loss in phase during a quantum measurement
thermodynamically equivalent to the magnitude information loss? (Doc.\,290)

\textbf{Continuous entropy flow.}
DRAM refresh costs: How exactly does the Landauer limit match measured
entropy flows in modern processors? Are there systematic
deviations due to quantum corrections?

\section{Open Question: Matzke's Axiom}

\textbf{Why $e_i^2 = +1$?}
In Matzke's hyperbit framework, it remains open why exactly this sign
forms the basis of physics. In the FFGFT, the analogous question is answered:
$\xi = 4/30000$ follows algebraically from the Galois group orders (Doc.\,338).
Matzke's open question has no direct counterpart in the FFGFT.

\section{Experimental Approaches}

Three experimental lines are particularly promising:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Bérut-type experiments} at the p-bit scale: Can one directly measure Landauer's limit
    at $L_8 \approx 22\,\text{nm}$ and room temperature?
  \item \textbf{Qubit decoherence calorimetry}: Does the heat generation during
    decoherence match the Landauer formula?
  \item \textbf{Galois spectroscopy}: Does $\mathrm{GF}(27)$ structure in
    particle spectra (Doc.\,356--357) show measurable signatures?
\end{enumerate}
