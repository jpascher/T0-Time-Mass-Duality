% Use of AI Tools (exactly as A010/A271)

This book was created using AI-supported tools.
The questions, the stipulations and all substantive decisions
come from the author; the tools formulate, calculate and create
test scripts. Responsibility for content and errors lies entirely
with the author.

\medskip\noindent
\textbf{Specifically:} Structure, organization, derivation steps, layer markings
(\SETZ, \BB, \QQ, \Skizze, \HH) and all substantive decisions
--- what is asserted, what is considered open, what is rejected ---
come from the primary documents of the FFGFT corpus and from the author.
AI tools have generated coherent prose and LaTeX code from them
as well as in individual cases suggested formulations that the author
has reviewed and adopted or rejected.

\medskip\noindent
\textbf{Reference:} The detailed wording of this convention is in
Doc.\,A010 (\textit{Purpose and Structure of the A-Series}),
\href{https://github.com/jpascher/T0-Time-Mass-Duality/blob/main/A_Serie_Export/pdf/A010_Zweck_Aufbau_De.pdf}{A010 (PDF)}.
