\section{Occasion}

Doc.~374 set up the bridge equations to OPH and carried the identification
$E_\star=E_P$ as a reading. Its result section states that \#740 is closed
under that reading. Two things have changed since: the coefficient has been
derived on the OPH side, and OPH has set out the status of \#740 in more
detail. This document records both and corrects the statement that went too
far.

\section{The theorem \QM}

Two observers read the same stress and geometry configuration at the same
metric norm. The tensor relation is $F_i=\kappa\,T_i+\varphi\,n_i$ with unknown
$\kappa$ and a common offset $\varphi$. On the entropy side there are a bulk
variation $b\,K_i\,T_i$, an area variation $-K_i(F_i-\psi\,n_i)$ and
stationarity $\Delta S_i+(q/L^2)\,\Delta A_i=0$. If the two observers see
different stress, $T_1\neq T_2$, then, with nothing assumed about the offsets,
\begin{equation}
  \kappa=\frac{b\,L^2}{q}.
\end{equation}
With $b=2\pi$ from the cap modular flow and $q=1/4$ from the area law this
gives $\kappa=8\pi L^2$ \KM, and from $\kappa=8\pi G$ it follows that $G=L^2$.

\section{Consequence for the reference energy}

With $L=\sqrt G$ (in units $\hbar=c=1$),
\begin{equation}
  E_\star=\frac{\hbar c}{L}=\sqrt{\frac{\hbar c^5}{G}}=E_P ,
\end{equation}
the non-reduced Planck energy \BM. The reduced reading is off by
$\sqrt{8\pi}=5.013$ and would require a different normalisation of the area
law; it is therefore not a choice of units but a different physical
assumption.

The reading from Doc.~374 is thus no longer posited on the OPH side but
derived. The prefactor $8\pi$ stays where it always was, in Einstein's
equation; the quarter in the area law selects $G$.

\section{Correction to Doc.~374}

Doc.~374 concludes that \#740 is closed under the reading $E_\star=E_P$. This
is withdrawn and corrected directly in Doc.~374. OPH carries \#740 as the whole common-world join and names eight
of fifteen dependencies as open \QM. Open there are in particular:

\begin{enumerate}\itemsep2pt
\item The length in the area law and the length in the pixel are so far the
  same by declaration; deriving them as one length on one source family is
  planned.
\item The tick. $\hbar c/\ell_P$ is an energy; a clock requires an evolving
  state, a gap and a readout, plus the map to a laboratory transition. On the
  FFGFT side $\tilde T\cdot m=1$ supplies the tick directly (Doc.~374, B3); on
  the OPH side it is work in progress.
\item The matching factor $m=v_F/v_{\rm source}=0.9984$, that is, the
  $0.16\,\%$. It is to be computed from the same matter action.
\end{enumerate}

What holds is therefore: the coefficient is derived, the bridge equations
B1--B4 of Doc.~374 stand unchanged, and the absolute scale follows through the
FFGFT chain. \#740 itself remains open.
