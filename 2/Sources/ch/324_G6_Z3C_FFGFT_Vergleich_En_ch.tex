\subsection{Matzke's G(6,$\mathbb{Z}_3\mathbb{C}$) Vacuum Structure}

\textbf{[S]} Matzke constructs the physical vacuum state $V$ as an
algebraic object in $G(6, \mathbb{Z}_3\mathbb{C})$, a Clifford algebra
over the coefficient field
$\mathbb{Z}_3\mathbb{C} = \{-1, 0, +1, \pm i\}$.
The central vacuum relation is
\begin{equation}
  V^2 = -V \quad (\text{in } \mathbb{Z}_3\mathbb{C}\text{ arithmetic}),
  \label{eq:vakrel}
\end{equation}
where $2 \equiv -1 \pmod{3}$. Three nilpotent vector pairs
$N_k^{\pm} = e_a \pm i\,e_b$ with $(N_k^\pm)^2 = 0$ decompose the
$\mathbb{C}^6$ spinor space into three eigensectors.
The Trine theorem states: for every nilpotent $N$ with $N^2 = 0$,
\begin{equation}
  T = \mathbf{1} + (\omega-1)N \quad \Rightarrow \quad T^3 = \mathbf{1}.
  \label{eq:trine}
\end{equation}

\subsection{The Quadratic Casimir Operator}

\textbf{[B]} The quadratic Casimir operator of a Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g}$
is the group invariant
\begin{equation}
  C_2(R) = \sum_{a=1}^{\dim\mathfrak{g}} T_a^{(R)} T_a^{(R)},
  \label{eq:casimir_def}
\end{equation}
where $T_a^{(R)}$ are the generators in representation $R$.
For $SU(N_c)$ in the \emph{fundamental} representation:
\begin{equation}
  C_2(SU(N_c)_{\text{fund}}) = \frac{N_c^2 - 1}{2N_c}.
  \label{eq:casimir_SUN}
\end{equation}
With $N_c = 3$ (algebraically necessary, Doc.~321, \textbf{[B]}):
\begin{equation}
  C_2(SU(3)_{\text{fund}})
  = \frac{9 - 1}{6} = \frac{8}{6} = \frac{4}{3}.
  \label{eq:casimir_SU3}
\end{equation}
