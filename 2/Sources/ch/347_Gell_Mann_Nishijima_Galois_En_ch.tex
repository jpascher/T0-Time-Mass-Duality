\section*{Abstract}

The Gell-Mann--Nishijima relation $Q=I_3+Y/2$ is derived from two already
established Galois properties: (1)~Hypercharge $Y=-1+\tfrac{4}{3}\mathrm{NQR}_{13}$
from the Legendre symbol mod~13 (Doc.~346, Theorem~A). (2)~Isospin
$I_3=\pm\tfrac{1}{2}$ from the Su/Sd split via the jE7 projector (Doc.~344,
Theorem~E). The combination gives all four fermion charges
$\{0,-1,+\tfrac{2}{3},-\tfrac{1}{3}\}$ exactly, without free parameters.
Theorem~E~[\SM] of Doc.~346 is thereby closed: \textbf{[B]}.

\section{Theorem A --- Hypercharge from QR/NQR}

\begin{theorem}[Hypercharge $Y$ from Legendre symbol \BM]
\[
  Y = -1 + \frac{4}{3}\cdot\mathrm{NQR}_{13},\qquad
  \mathrm{NQR}_{13} = \begin{cases}
    0 & k\bmod13\in\mathrm{QR}_{13}\quad(\text{leptons})\\
    1 & k\bmod13\in\mathrm{NQR}_{13}\quad(\text{quarks})
  \end{cases}
\]
gives $Y_\mathrm{lepton}=-1$ and $Y_\mathrm{quark}=+\tfrac{1}{3}$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Leptons (QR orbits $O_1,O_3$): $\mathrm{NQR}=0\Rightarrow Y=-1+0=-1$ \checkmark\\
Quarks (NQR orbits $O_2,O_4$): $\mathrm{NQR}=1\Rightarrow Y=-1+\tfrac{4}{3}=\tfrac{1}{3}$ \checkmark \qed
\end{proof}

\textbf{Connection to Doc.~346 \BM.} The NQR bit is exactly the Legendre symbol
$\left(\tfrac{k}{13}\right)=-1$ --- the same property classifying electric
neutrality in Doc.~346. Hypercharge and electric neutrality have the same
algebraic source.

\section{Theorem B --- Isospin from Su/Sd split}

The Su/Sd separation by the jE7 projector (Doc.~344, Theorem~E) is exactly the

\section{What remains open: SU$(2)_L$ and CKM \SM}

\textbf{SU$(2)_L$ chirality.}
Chirality requires $\gamma_5=e_1\cdots e_6\in\mathrm{Cl}(6)$.
The $\mathbb{Z}_2$ parity in GF$(27)^*$ separates particles from anti-particles
($k<13$ vs.\ $k\geq13$), not left-handed from right-handed.
All four primitive orbits $O_1\ldots O_4$ are same-chiral.
Needed: the bridge GF$(27)^*\to\mathrm{Cl}(6)\xrightarrow{\gamma_5}\mathrm{chirality}$.
Begun in Docs.~341/344, not yet complete. \SM

\textbf{CKM mixing angles.}
GF$(27)$ carries a trace bilinear form
$\langle a,b\rangle=\mathrm{Tr}_{\mathrm{GF}(27)/\mathrm{GF}(3)}(ab)$.
The Cabibbo angle between generations would be the overlap
$|\langle O_{2,\mathrm{Gen.1}},\,O_{4,\mathrm{Gen.2}}\rangle|/(\|O_2\|\|O_4\|)$.
