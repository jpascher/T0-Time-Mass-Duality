\section*{Abstract}

We examine how far the Gell-Mann--Nishijima relation $Q=I_3+Y/2$ follows from two
already established Galois properties. (1)~Isospin $I_3=\pm\tfrac{1}{2}$ follows from
the Su/Sd split via the jE7 projector (Doc.~344, Theorem~E) \BM.
(2)~Hypercharge $Y$ is \emph{not} fixed by the QR/NQR bit mod~13 (Doc.~346) alone:
charged leptons and quarks are both NQR, and the lepton doublet $(\nu,e)_L$ lies on
both sides of the bit (Theorem~A, \SM). With the SM values $Y=-1$ (lepton doublet)
and $Y=+\tfrac{1}{3}$ (quark doublet) the relation gives the four fermion charges
$\{0,-1,+\tfrac{2}{3},-\tfrac{1}{3}\}$; Theorem~E of Doc.~346 is thereby closed for
$I_3$ and open for $Y$ \SM\ (Doc.~190, R146).
\textit{(Corrected on 1~Oct.~2026: previously ``$Y=-1+\tfrac{4}{3}\mathrm{NQR}_{13}$ from the Legendre symbol'' and ``Theorem~E \ldots{} is thereby closed: [B]'' -- see Theorem~A.)}

\section{Background}

Doc.~346 established the fermion classification from two Galois properties:
QR/NQR bit (electric neutrality) and $N\bmod3$ (colour charge). Theorem~E~[\SM]
left open: does $Q=I_3+Y/2$ also follow from Galois structure?

\section{Theorem A --- Hypercharge from QR/NQR}

\begin{theorem}[Hypercharge $Y$ is not fixed by the QR/NQR bit alone \SM]
By Doc.~346 (Theorem~B and orbit table): QR orbits $O_1=\{1,3,9\}$ $=$ neutrinos,
$O_3=\{4,10,12\}$ $=$ antineutrinos; NQR orbits $O_4=\{7,8,11\}$ $=$ charged leptons,
$O_2=\{2,5,6\}$ $=$ quarks. There is no function $Y=f(\mathrm{NQR}_{13})$ reproducing
the SM values $Y=-1$ (lepton doublet $(\nu,e)_L$) and $Y=+\tfrac{1}{3}$ (quark doublet
$(u,d)_L$). The assignment of $Y$ must also include $N\bmod3$ and the
particle/antiparticle distinction; it is open (Doc.~190, R146).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Assume $Y=f(\mathrm{NQR}_{13})$.
Neutrino ($O_1$, QR): $f(0)=-1$.
Electron ($O_4$, NQR), in the same doublet as the neutrino: $f(1)=-1$.
Up quark ($O_2$, NQR): $f(1)=+\tfrac{1}{3}$ --- contradiction with $f(1)=-1$.
Charged leptons and quarks both lie on the NQR side, the lepton doublet $(\nu,e)_L$
on both sides of the bit; the bit therefore neither separates leptons from quarks nor
keeps the doublet partners together. In addition, the QR orbit $O_3$ (antineutrinos)
carries the opposite sign of $Y$ to $O_1$, although both are QR. \qed
\end{proof}
\textit{(Corrected on 1~Oct.~2026: previously Theorem~A \BM: $Y=-1+\tfrac{4}{3}\mathrm{NQR}_{13}$ with QR $=$ leptons ($O_1,O_3$) and NQR $=$ quarks ($O_2,O_4$), proof by substitution -- contradicts the orbit assignment of Doc.~346; consequences see note below.)}

\textbf{Connection to Doc.~346.} The NQR bit is exactly the Legendre symbol
$\left(\tfrac{k}{13}\right)=-1$ --- the same property classifying electric
neutrality in Doc.~346 \BM. Hypercharge does not follow from it (Theorem~A) \SM.
\textit{(Note of 1~Oct.~2026: with the earlier version of Theorem~A the electron ($O_4$, NQR) would receive $Y = +1/3$ and $Q = -\tfrac12 + \tfrac16 = -\tfrac13$ instead of $-1$, and the antineutrinos ($O_3$, QR) would receive $Y = -1$ instead of $+1$. The SM values $Y = -1$ for left-handed lepton doublets and $+1/3$ for quark doublets are themselves correct.)}

\section{Theorem B --- Isospin from Su/Sd split}

\begin{theorem}[Isospin $I_3$ from Su/Sd split \BM]
\[
  I_3 = \begin{cases}
    +\tfrac{1}{2} & \text{Su sector (Doc.~344: }F\cdot jE7=+F\text{)}\\
    -\tfrac{1}{2} & \text{Sd sector (Doc.~344: }F\cdot jE7=-F\text{)}
  \end{cases}
\]
\end{theorem}

The Su/Sd separation by the jE7 projector (Doc.~344, Theorem~E) is exactly the
doublet splitting of weak isospin. No new assumption --- the jE7 projector was
already established in the GALG/Furey construction.

\section{What remains open: SU$(2)_L$ and CKM \SM}

\textbf{Hypercharge.} $Y$ from $N\bmod3$ and the particle/antiparticle distinction (Theorem~A). \SM

\textbf{SU$(2)_L$ chirality.}
Chirality requires $\gamma_5=e_1\cdots e_6\in\mathrm{Cl}(6)$.
The $\mathbb{Z}_2$ parity in GF$(27)^*$ separates particles from anti-particles
($k<13$ vs.\ $k\geq13$), not left-handed from right-handed.
All four primitive orbits $O_1\ldots O_4$ are same-chiral.
Needed: the bridge GF$(27)^*\to\mathrm{Cl}(6)\xrightarrow{\gamma_5}\mathrm{chirality}$.
Begun in Docs.~341/344, not yet complete. \SM

\textbf{CKM mixing angles.}
GF$(27)$ carries a trace bilinear form
$\langle a,b\rangle=\mathrm{Tr}_{\mathrm{GF}(27)/\mathrm{GF}(3)}(ab)$.
The Cabibbo angle between generations would be the overlap
$|\langle O_{2,\mathrm{Gen.1}},\,O_{4,\mathrm{Gen.2}}\rangle|/(\|O_2\|\|O_4\|)$.
Experimental: $\sin\theta_C\approx0.225$; Galois candidate $\sin(\pi/14)\approx0.222$ ($-1.2\,\%$).
Three checked directions: (1)~direct $\xi$-corrections: ratio $\approx1685$, no mechanism.
(2)~$\xi^{|k_i-k_j|}$ weighting: values $\sim10^{-8}$, no mechanism.
(3)~Galois quotients: $2/9\approx0.222$ ($-1.4\,\%$), $3/13\approx0.231$ ($+2.4\,\%$),
near but not forced. Full derivation requires its own document. \SM
