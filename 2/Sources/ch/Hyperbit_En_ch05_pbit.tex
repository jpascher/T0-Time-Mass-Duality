\section{The Operating Point Between Bit and Qubit}

The classical bit requires a deep potential well $U \gg k_B T$ ---
robust over years, without cooling. The qubit operates at the opposite
extreme: metastable torsion, coherence in the millisecond range, enforced
by cooling to mK temperatures.

The \textbf{p-bit} occupies the conceptually previously unexplored
intermediate range: a magnetic nanostructure in which the
torsion barrier becomes thermally surmountable. The system switches spontaneously
between the two macrostates.
