\section*{Selected External Sources}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=3.5em,style=sameline,itemsep=0.4em]\small

\item[Bell 1964] J.~S. Bell: \textit{On the Einstein-Podolsky-Rosen
  paradox.} Physics 1 (3), 195–200 (1964). — The original argument
  for the impossibility of local-realistic hidden variables.

\item[CHSH 1969] J.~F. Clauser, M.~A. Horne, A.~Shimony, R.~A. Holt:
  \textit{Proposed experiment to test local hidden-variable theories.}
  Phys. Rev. Lett. 23, 880 (1969). — Experimentally testable form of the
  Bell inequality.

\item[Aspect 1982] A.~Aspect, P.~Grangier, G.~Roger: \textit{Experimental
  realization of Einstein-Podolsky-Rosen-Bohm Gedankenexperiment.}
  Phys. Rev. Lett. 49, 91 (1982). — First loophole-free
  Bell test (loopholes fully closed in 2015).

\item[Kochen/Specker 1967] S.~Kochen, E.~P. Specker: \textit{The
  problem of hidden variables in quantum mechanics.} J. Math. Mech.
  17, 59–87 (1967). — Proof of the context-dependence of observables.

\item[PBR 2012] M.~F. Pusey, J.~Barrett, T.~Rudolph: \textit{On the
  reality of the quantum state.} Nature Physics 8, 475–478 (2012). —
  Shows: the quantum state is ontic, not a state of knowledge.

\item[Shor 1994] P.~W. Shor: \textit{Algorithms for quantum computation:
  discrete logarithms and factoring.} Proc. 35th FOCS, 124–134 (1994). —
  Polynomial-time factorization on a quantum computer.

\item[Grover 1996] L.~K. Grover: \textit{A fast quantum mechanical
  algorithm for database search.} Proc. 28th STOC, 212–219 (1996). —
  $O(\sqrt N)$ search in an unsorted database.

\item[Loss/DiVincenzo 1998] D.~Loss, D.~P. DiVincenzo: \textit{Quantum
  computation with quantum dots.} Phys. Rev. A 57, 120 (1998). —
  Spin-qubit gates via exchange interaction.

\item[Willow 2024] Google Quantum AI: \textit{Quantum error correction
  below the surface code threshold.} Nature 634, 2024. — First
  surpassing of the error correction threshold on Willow
  (105 qubits, IBM Heron comparison).

\item[Will 2025] Will et al.: \textit{Floquet topological order in a
  quantum processor.} Nature, Sept. 2025. — Non-equilibrium
  topological order (chiral edge modes, anyons) on Willow.

\item[Solovay/Kitaev] A.~Y. Kitaev: \textit{Quantum computations:
  algorithms and error correction.} Russ. Math. Surv. 52 (1997). —
  Universality of $\{H,T,\mathrm{CNOT}\}$ for quantum computing.

\item[Tsirelson 1980] B.~S. Tsirelson: \textit{Quantum generalizations
  of Bell's inequality.} Lett. Math. Phys. 4, 93–100 (1980). —
  Upper bound of quantum correlations: $2\sqrt2$.

\item[Kaluza 1921 / Klein 1926] T.~Kaluza (1921), O.~Klein (1926):
  Compactified dimension generates discrete mode tower and
  charge quantization. Mathematical blueprint for the
  time-mass mechanism of the FFGFT.

\item[Page/Wootters 1983] D.~N. Page, W.~K. Wootters: \textit{Evolution
  without evolution.} Phys. Rev. D 27, 2885 (1983). — Time as
  entanglement correlation within the state space.

\item[Weyl 1912] H.~Weyl: Eigenvalue law of compact manifolds
  $N(\lambda)\sim C\lambda^{d/2}$. Establishes the impossibility
  of carrying Riemann zeros as a Laplace spectrum.

\item[Inagaki 2016 / McMahon 2016] T.~Inagaki et al.; P.~L. McMahon
  et al.: Coherent Ising Machine (CIM) with DOPO network. Science
  2016. — Experimental realization of physical optimization
  through relaxation.

\item[Schiemann 1997] A.~Schiemann: Isospectral, non-isometric
  tori in dimension 4. Shows that the spectrum alone does not determine the lattice.
  Limit of the informative value of spectral methods.

\item[BSI 2023] Federal Office for Information Security:
  \textit{Status of quantum computer development.} 2023. —
  Current assessment of the threat to cryptographic methods.

\end{description}
