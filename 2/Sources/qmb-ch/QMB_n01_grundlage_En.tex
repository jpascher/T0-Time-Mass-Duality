\chapter{The Geometric Foundation}
\label{ch:grundlage}

The fundamental relation of the FFGFT is
\begin{equation}
  \tilde T \cdot m = 1,
  \label{eq:tm}
\end{equation}
where $\tilde T$ is a compact time coordinate and $m$ is the mass of a
mode.\status{SETZUNG} This relation is not a derived
equation, but the geometric postulate from which everything else
follows.

\section{The Carrier: $T^4/\mathbb{Z}_3$}

The FFGFT operates on a compact torus $T^4 = (S^1)^4$ with a
$\mathbb{Z}_3$ lattice symmetry. This carrier has two essential
properties.

\textbf{Compactness.} Each coordinate is periodic. This has an
immediate consequence: The admissible modes are discrete — not because
nature is postulated to be discrete, but because periodicity
forces discrete Fourier modes. The Fourier modes of the carrier yield
exactly the particle spectra of the Standard Model.\status{K}

\textbf{$\mathbb{Z}_3$ symmetry.} The quotienting by
$\mathbb{Z}_3$ creates nine fixed points and a triality symmetry with
$|\mathrm{Aut}(D_4)| = 1152 = 2^7\cdot3^2$. This symmetry becomes
visible in the spectral operator and controls which modes are stable.\status{B}

\section{The Structure Parameter $\xi$}

The only free parameter of the theory is
\begin{equation}
  \xi = \frac{4}{30000} = \frac{1}{7500} \approx 1{,}333\times10^{-4}.
\end{equation}
It is not a calibration quantity, but follows from the
Galois group orders of the carrier: Specifically,
$\xi = C_2(SU(3)_{\mathrm{fund}})/N_{\mathrm{Fourier}}= (4/3)/10^4$.\status{K}
(Doc.~338, Doc.~353)

All masses, couplings, and corrections of the FFGFT can be derived from $\xi$;
SI anchor values (electron mass, $H_0$) serve only for
conversion into SI units, not for derivation.

\section{The Relation $\tilde T\cdot m = 1$ and Its Consequences}

Equation~\eqref{eq:tm} has three immediate consequences.

\textbf{Mode-bound time scale.} Each mode with mass $m_i$ carries
its own compact time scale $\tilde T_i = 1/m_i$.\status{B} Time
is not an external parameter, but a mode-specific property of the
carrier. The Pauli theorem and the missing time operator of standard QM
are not a problem in this reading: On a compact carrier, the
Pauli argument does not apply (Doc.~334).

\textbf{Mass arithmetic from geometry.} The ratio
$(m_\mu/m_e)^2 = 43200$ follows both geometrically from the
winding numbers and from the Galois group orders
$|GF(9)^*|^2\cdot 5^2\cdot|GF(27)|$.\status{K} (Doc.~338)

\textbf{Fractal correction.} The transition from the compact carrier to the
SI scale brings a recursive correction
$K_{\mathrm{frak}} = 1 - 100\xi \approx 0{,}9867$, which follows from the
Type III pullback structure of the carrier.\status{B} (Doc.~333)
