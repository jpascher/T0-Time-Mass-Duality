## Marker system — specification additions

### R103 — Derivational closure condition for [K] and [B] explicitly declared

*Occasion: correspondence with S. Vossen, August 2026
(thread: "substrate standing and ontological constitution").*

A result carries **[K]** or **[B]** only if its complete derivational chain from ξ
is traceable without gap. Admissible chain links:

1. Geometric consequences of the fixed T⁴/ℤ₃ structure
2. Algebraic consequences of a preceding chain link
3. Declared external inputs ([SETZUNG]) including PDG values and SI quantities,
   which are themselves ξ-derived quantities in SI representation

A result that requires an external parameter not so anchored carries neither [K]
nor [B], regardless of whether it reaches a correct numerical value.

This condition was consistently observed in practice throughout the corpus (cf.
Docs. 320, 323, 333, 335, 339) but had not been explicitly declared as a separate
criterion in the marker definitions. Future document-local marker declarations
should state the closure condition explicitly; Doc. 190 records the occasion and
date.

**Corollary — two kinds of closure:**

- *ξ-internal closure* — every quantity is generated internally from ξ, no external
  input; applies where achievable; [K] without [SETZUNG] in the path.
- *Auditable closure within a declared framework* — external standard or empirical
  inputs are admitted, provided every dependency is localised and declared as
  [SETZUNG] or cited as [Q]. Applies in documents such as A155 and Doc. 335.

**Corollary — path-dependence of [S]:**

An open [S] point suspends [K] or [B] standing only where the derivational path of
that result passes through the unresolved [S] point. Contamination is
path-dependent, not global through the document or corpus.
